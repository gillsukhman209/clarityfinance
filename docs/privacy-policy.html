Clarity Finance Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Clarity Finance helps users understand their spending, connected accounts, credit card balances, recurring charges, payment due dates, trends, and notifications. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Clarity Finance may collect or process:

  • Account information from connected financial accounts, such as account name, institution, account type, balance, credit limit, due date, and minimum payment amount.
  • Transaction information, such as merchant name, amount, date, category, transaction status, and account identifier.
  • Recurring charge information generated from your transaction history, such as subscriptions, recurring bills, charge frequency, and confidence labels.
  • Apple Card statement data if you manually import Apple Card PDF statements.
  • Apple Wallet financial data if you choose to grant access through Apple FinanceKit, where available.
  • Authentication information, such as your Sign in with Apple user identifier and email address when provided by Apple.
  • Push notification information, such as your device push token and notification preferences.
  • Basic diagnostics needed to debug account connection, sync, and notification issues.

How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Show your accounts, balances, credit cards, transactions, subscriptions, bills, trends, and spending summaries.
  • Help explain credit card payment details, due dates, minimum payments, utilization, and interest risk.
  • Generate spending insights and simple financial coaching inside the app.
  • Send optional push notifications about notable spending or trends, if you enable notifications.
  • Sync and restore your account data across app installs when you are signed in.
  • Improve reliability, troubleshoot bugs, and protect the app from abuse.

Financial Account Connections

Clarity Finance uses Plaid to connect supported banks and credit cards. When you connect an account through Plaid, your bank login credentials are handled by Plaid. Clarity Finance does not receive or store your bank username or password.

For eligible Apple Wallet financial accounts, Clarity Finance may use Apple FinanceKit with your permission. FinanceKit access is controlled by Apple and your device permissions. We only use this data to power Clarity Finance features that you choose to use.

Apple Card PDF Imports

If you import Apple Card PDF statements, Clarity Finance extracts transaction and statement data from those files so the app can show Apple Card transactions and recurring charges. Only import statements you want Clarity Finance to process.

AI Features

Clarity Finance may use AI services to classify transactions, detect recurring charges, clean merchant names, and generate spending insights. We send only the information needed for those features, such as merchant names, transaction amounts, dates, categories, and account context. We do not send bank login credentials.

Notifications

If you enable viral spending notifications, Clarity Finance stores your device push token and notification preferences so we can send optional alerts. You can turn off notifications in the app or in iOS Settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal financial data. We share data only with service providers needed to operate the app, such as Plaid for account connections, Supabase for authentication and database services, Vercel for backend hosting, Apple for FinanceKit and push notification services, and AI providers for optional classification and insight features.

Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards to protect your data, including secure transport, platform keychain storage where appropriate, backend access controls, and provider security features. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and limit access to what is needed.

Your Choices

  • You can choose whether to connect Plaid accounts, grant FinanceKit access, import Apple Card PDFs, enable AI scanning, or enable notifications.
  • You can remove connected accounts in the app. Removing an account removes app data linked to that account.
  • You can disable notifications in the app or in system settings.
  • You can sign out to stop account-based sync on that device.

Children

Clarity Finance is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. If we make material changes, we will update the date above and provide notice where required.
